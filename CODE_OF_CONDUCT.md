# Code of Conduct

Qlossary follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or
level of knowledge. Beginners in quantum computing are especially welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its players.
- Credit other people's work and sources.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Offensive names on the leaderboard or in contributions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent
the project. Report problems privately to the maintainer,
[@saadbhattii](https://github.com/saadbhattii), for example through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/saadbhattii/Qlossary/security/advisories/new)
if you need a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban
participants who break this code.
